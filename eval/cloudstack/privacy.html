1. Information Collection

CloudStack ("we") may collect the following types of information:

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you register a CloudStack account, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, and password.
  • Identity Verification Information: As required by Chinese laws and regulations, we collect identity information, such as identity card or passport numbers, for identity verification.
  • Payment Information: When you purchase services, we collect payment information, such as bank card details or an Alipay account.
  • Contact Records: When you contact customer support, we record your contact details and the content of your communication.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: This includes your IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Usage Logs: This includes pages visited, links clicked, search history, and interaction times.
  • Location Information: We may determine your approximate location using your IP address or other technologies.

2. Information Use

We may use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving our services;
  • Processing registration, verification, and transaction requests;
  • Sending service-related notices, bills, and reminders;
  • Providing customer support and technical services;
  • Performing security checks and identity verification;
  • Analyzing usage patterns to improve products and services;
  • Complying with laws, regulations, and regulatory requirements;
  • Other purposes to which you consent.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share it in the following circumstances:

  • Affiliates: Sharing with CloudStack affiliates as necessary to provide services.
  • Service Providers: Sharing with providers acting on our behalf, such as payment processors, data analysts, and customer support providers.
  • Legal Requirements: As required by laws, regulations, court orders, or government authorities.
  • Protection of Rights: To protect the rights, property, and safety of CloudStack, our users, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: User information may be included in transferred assets during a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.
Important: We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Information Security

We use a range of security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Technical Measures: We use SSL/TLS encryption in transit, encrypted storage, access controls, and other safeguards.
  • Administrative Measures: We maintain strict data-management procedures and restrict employee access to personal information.
  • Physical Measures: Data centers use access-control systems, monitoring equipment, and other physical safeguards.
  • Incident Response: We maintain incident-response plans to respond to and address data-security incidents promptly.

Although we take reasonable security measures, the internet is not 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws and regulations, you have the following rights:

  • Access: You may access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Deletion: In certain circumstances, you may request deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: You may withdraw your previously given consent at any time.
  • Account Closure: You may request closure of your CloudStack account at any time.
  • Complaints: You may complain to a regulator if you believe we have infringed your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9 of this policy.

6. Use of Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your sign-in status and preferences;
  • Analyze website usage and improve services;
  • Provide personalized content and advertising;
  • Keep the website secure and prevent fraud.

You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings, but doing so may affect some service features.

7. Protection of Minors

We take the protection of minors' personal information seriously:

  • Our services are primarily intended for adults and are not actively offered to minors.
  • If you are a minor, read this policy with a guardian and use our services only with the guardian's consent.
  • If we learn that a minor's personal information was collected without prior guardian consent, we will delete it as soon as possible.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time:

  • We will publish the updated policy on the website with its update date.
  • We will notify you of material changes through website notices, email, or other appropriate channels.
  • Continuing to use our services indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

Filing Process

1

Enter Information

Complete the filing form and upload supporting documents.

2

Initial Review

CloudStack performs an initial review (1 business day).

3

Authority Review

Submit to the communications authority (5-20 business days).

4

Filing Complete

Receive a filing number and make the website available.

📋 Filing Notes

  • Filing information must be accurate; false information causes rejection.
  • During review, the website may remain accessible with a filing-in-progress notice.
  • After approval, display the filing number in the homepage footer and link to the authority website.
  • Update the filing promptly when its information changes.
  • Cancel the filing when it is no longer needed.
